### Examples and Resources for Computational Color

- [Color Models and Color Spaces](https://programmingdesignsystems.com/color/color-models-and-color-spaces/index.html)

- [Color Schemes](https://programmingdesignsystems.com/color/color-schemes/index.html#color-schemes-dV9Rf6L)

- [Standard HTML Color Pickers](https://www.w3schools.com/colors/colors_picker.asp)

[Kimiko Ryokai's I/O Brush (2004)](https://tangible.media.mit.edu/project/io-brush/)

[CHI paper on ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/citation.cfm?id=985731)

[Playful Palette](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kapQfn7K0fQ&ab_channel=MashaShugrina) by Masha Shugrina et al.

[SIGGRAPH paper on ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/citation.cfm?id=985731https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3072959.3073690)

[Live Demo](https://colorsails.com/build)

[Tauba Aurerbach's RGB Colorspace Atlas (2011)](http://taubaauerbach.com/view.php?id=286&alt=2948)

[Adobe Kuler](https://color.adobe.com/explore)
Enables constraining colors across different rule sets- i.e triad, monochromatic, complementary etc.

[The Color Project at IFP Media Center by MPC New York](https://vimeo.com/84711932)
Created by MPC New York in collaboration with artist Thompson Harrell, ‘The Color Project’ explores narratives through colour, line, and form found in geographic satellite imagery. Utilizing Google Earth together with custom software, the install spans over 27 HD screens pointing locations of the films that have been produced by IFP.

Ghita Jalal, Nolwenn Maudet and Wendy E. Mackay's Color Portraits (2015) [CHI paper on ACM Digital Library](https://doi.org/10.1145/2702123.2702173)[Video Demonstration on Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/217826074)
